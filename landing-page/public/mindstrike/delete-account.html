Delete your account

Your account. Your decision.

Effective and last updated: 20 September 2026

Permanently remove your Mindstrike account and the progress associated with it. You can start deletion inside the app without contacting support.

Delete from Mindstrike

  1. Open Mindstrike and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the Account tab and scroll to Privacy & support.
  3. Choose Delete account and review what will be removed.
  4. Confirm Delete permanently. If the account is linked to Apple, complete the Apple verification prompt.
  5. Keep an internet connection until the app confirms deletion and returns to sign-in.

If a request fails, the app shows an error so you can retry. Do not assume deletion succeeded until you see confirmation.

What is removed

  • Your Mindstrike sign-in account and linked provider identities in our authentication service.
  • Your cloud profile, rating, coins, ranked results, practice progress and unfinished sessions.
  • Your account’s cloud recovery snapshots and associated queue and live-duel records.
  • The deleted account’s cached progress, old pre-account saves and sign-in session on the iPhone making the request. Other signed-in players’ saved profiles are preserved.

What deletion does not do

It does not delete your Apple or Google account, another Mindstrike player’s account, or files in backups you control. Your identity is anonymised in opponents’ cloud results, preserving their scores. Their offline caches may still contain what they previously saw until they synchronise. Other devices can retain offline copies until removed. Service backups, security logs and any records we must retain by law are covered by our retention policy.

Deletion is permanent. A later sign-in creates a fresh Mindstrike account. This version has no subscriptions to cancel. Signing out, removing the app, or revoking Apple/Google access alone does not delete your Mindstrike cloud account.

Cannot open your account?

If you cannot sign in, cannot find the deletion control in your installed version, or need help with an earlier request, contact ashishume@gmail.com with the subject “Mindstrike account deletion”. Include your Mindstrike player ID or account email and which sign-in provider you used. With Apple Hide My Email, include the relay address shown in your Apple account settings if available. Do not send passwords, tokens or verification codes.

We will verify ownership before removing an account. Emailing opens a support request; it does not instantly delete an account. We will explain the next steps and confirm when it is completed.
